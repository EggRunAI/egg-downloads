# Start here

Nothing here is fixed. Add eggs from the hub (⌘K), remove the ones you don't want.

An **egg** is a complete computer in a file. You download one, double-click it, and a whole Linux desktop opens in a window on your Mac, sealed off from everything else.

## 1. Download an egg

![Double-click an egg on the desk and watch the ring fill](images/download-egg.png)

The eggs on your desk are not on your Mac yet. Each one is a download: double-click it and the ring fills. The first one takes a few minutes; after that it starts in seconds.

[Download Diamond Linux now](egg:install/egg/diamond)

## 2. Open it

Double-click again once the ring is full. The desktop opens in its own window. Everything you do inside stays inside.

## 3. Make something

Diamond Linux comes with Claude and Codex on the dock. Click one, sign in with your own account, and ask it to build something. See **Vibe coding in Diamond**.
